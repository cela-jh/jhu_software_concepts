About

Cameron Ela (EE-LUH) is a Data Engineering Intern at CureMetrix. He earned his B.S. in Economics and Data Science with a Minor in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Southern California in 2024. Continuing at USC, he completed his M.S. in Applied Data Science a year later. He is based in Long Beach, California.

Cameron hopes to use his data science background to advance healthcare, helping develop more effective treatments for autoimmune diseases. Specifically, his goal is to one day help cure or permanently suppress ulcerative colitis, a form of the autoimmune condition inflammatory bowel disease.
